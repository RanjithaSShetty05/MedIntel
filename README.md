## 📖 About

**MedIntel** is an AI-powered **Clinical Triage & Decision Support Platform** designed to transform unstructured patient information into structured, accessible, and explainable clinical insights.

This repository contains the **React frontend** of the MedIntel platform.

The frontend provides a modern clinical workspace where healthcare professionals can interact with:

- 🔐 Authentication
- 🏥 Clinical cases
- 👤 Patients
- 📊 Dashboard analytics
- 🤖 AI-assisted clinical insights
- 🕒 Clinical timelines
- 📝 Doctor notes
- 💊 Drug safety information
- 📄 Clinical reports
- 🔎 Clinical search
- 🛡️ Audit and activity information

The frontend communicates with the MedIntel **Spring Boot REST API** and presents the resulting clinical information through a structured and user-friendly interface.

> ⚠️ **Responsible AI Notice:** MedIntel is a clinical decision-support system. AI-generated information is intended to assist qualified healthcare professionals and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or clinical judgment.
